## Wiring

### Servo
| Servo Wire | Connect To |
|-----------|-----------|
| Signal (orange/yellow) | Pin **9** |
| VCC (red) | 5V |
| GND (brown/black) | GND |

### RGB LED
Each colour leg goes through its own 220Ω resistor. The long leg (common cathode) goes to GND.

| LED Leg | Arduino Pin |
|---------|------------|
| Red | Pin **3** (PWM) |
| Green | Pin **5** (PWM) |
| Blue | Pin **6** (PWM) |
| Common | GND |

### Button
| Button Leg | Connect To |
|-----------|-----------|
| Leg 1 | Pin **2** |
| Leg 2 | GND |
